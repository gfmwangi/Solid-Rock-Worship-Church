ABOUT US

Solid Rock Worship Church House of Grace

Solid Rock Worship Church is a place where people come together to experience God's love, grow in their faith, and build meaningful relationships with Him and with one another.

We are a family, united by the love of Christ, committed to worship, prayer, and living out His Word in our daily lives.

Our mission is to help people find hope, discover their purpose, and become all that God has called them to be.

Whether you are a first-time visitor or a long-time member, you are always welcome here — because you belong.
